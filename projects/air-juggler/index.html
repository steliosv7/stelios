Air Juggler

Keep the ball in the air. Move the paddle with your pointer, touch, or ← / → keys. Camera mode tracks your index finger; video stays on your device. Camera mode downloads the MediaPipe library and model.

Score 0 · Lives 3

Ready. Press Start game to play without a camera.

Use good lighting and keep one hand visible. Tracking runs at up to 15 frames/second. HTTPS or localhost is required for camera access. No microphone is requested.
